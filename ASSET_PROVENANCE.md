# Asset provenance

Background and vehicle image assets used in SimMOT were created by the authors.

The released assets are used by the SimMOT generator to render the synthetic
top-down traffic scenes. They are not sourced from UAVDT, MOTChallenge, stock
asset libraries, or third-party vehicle-image collections.

## Licensing

Unless otherwise noted, the visual assets distributed with SimMOT were
created by the dataset author(s) and are released under the same dataset
license as SimMOT: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0).

No third-party visual assets are intentionally redistributed as part of the
SimMOT v1.0 release.
